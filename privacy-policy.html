🇮🇳 Privacy Policy — Azadi Tales

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Azadi Tales is an educational mobile app developed for Indian children. This privacy policy explains how the app handles user information.

2. Information We Collect

Azadi Tales does NOT collect any personal information. The only data stored by the app is:

  • Child's first name — stored locally on the device only
  • App progress (stories read, XP, badges) — stored locally on the device only
  • Avatar selection — stored locally on the device only

This data never leaves the device. It is not transmitted to any server, third party, or cloud service.

3. No Internet Required

Azadi Tales works fully offline. The app does not connect to the internet during normal use. No data is sent to or received from any external server.

4. Children's Privacy

Azadi Tales is designed for children. We take children's privacy very seriously.

  • We do not collect any personal information from children
  • We do not require account creation or registration
  • We do not serve advertisements
  • We do not share any data with third parties
  • We comply with applicable children's privacy laws

5. Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on the user's device using AsyncStorage. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data.

6. Third Party Services

Azadi Tales does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

7. Permissions

The app requests the following device permission:

  • Microphone — used only for the Battle Cry recording feature, where children record themselves shouting famous battle cries. Recordings are stored locally on the device and are never uploaded or shared.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
